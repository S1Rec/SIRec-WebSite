How it works

The SIRec team runs its own servers and DNS routing, so Rec Room traffic goes through the revival infrastructure.

Step 1

Switch your DNS

Set the SIRec DNS server in your device or router settings. Instructions are sent once your application is approved.

Step 2

Open Rec Room

No mods, no patches, no third-party launchers. Just the regular official app.

Step 3

Play the Revival

Your traffic is automatically routed to SIRec's servers and you land in the restored 2026 revival version.
